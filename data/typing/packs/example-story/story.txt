# An example text for a pack with order = text (written for ReWrithe, 2026-11-09). One line = one or more pieces.
The night shift began at ten.
Only the old guard was awake.
Rain tapped on the long windows.
A door opened at the end of the hall.
Nobody had a key to that door.
Footsteps came up from the cellar.
They were slow and very heavy.
The lights went out one by one.
A cold wind smelled of wet earth.
Something laughed in the dark.
The guard lit his lantern.
Eyes looked back at him from below.
He ran to the stairs and did not stop.
Behind him the house woke up.
Every room was full of shadows.
Every shadow was moving.
Morning was still six hours away.
